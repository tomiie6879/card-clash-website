Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Developer: Pham Dinh Loc

This policy explains how the independent developer of Card Clash handles information when you use the game and this website.

1. Who we are

Card Clash is developed and published by Pham Dinh Loc, an independent game developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, contact mrduck.sg@gmail.com.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features and services enabled in the released version of Card Clash, we may process:

  • Game progress and settings: player progress, unlocked content, currency balances, preferences and a player identifier. This information may be stored locally on your device.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system, app version, language, country or approximate location derived from IP address, crash data and performance diagnostics.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers, ad interactions and consent choices when advertising is enabled.
  • Purchase information: product identifiers, purchase status and transaction tokens. Payment card details are processed by Google Play and are not received by us.
  • Support communications: your email address and the information you provide when you contact support.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain and improve the game.
  • Save progress and deliver purchased or earned content.
  • Show and measure advertising, including rewarded ads.
  • Detect errors, fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising and consent

Card Clash may use Google Mobile Ads to display ads, including rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game benefit. Where required, we ask for consent before using personal data for advertising. You can review or change available privacy choices from the game’s settings. Ad providers may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics and ad interaction data under their own policies.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Google may collect payment and account information under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive purchase confirmation and identifiers needed to deliver and restore eligible purchases.

6. Service providers

We may use service providers such as Google Play, Google Mobile Ads, Unity and crash or analytics services included in the released app. These providers process information on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own terms. The exact providers and data practices will be reflected in the app’s Google Play Data safety disclosure.

7. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. Service providers retain information according to their policies. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain records and comply with law.

8. Children’s privacy

Card Clash is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or object to certain processing. You may reset or limit your advertising identifier through Android settings and manage available consent choices in the game. Contact us to make a privacy request.

10. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to an independent mobile game. No system is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to protections required by applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, service providers or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date.
